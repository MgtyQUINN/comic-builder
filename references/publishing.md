# Publishing and print

## Copyright (important for commercial release)

In the US Copyright Office's current view (report on copyright and AI, part 2, January 2025),
images generated by AI from a prompt alone are not protected. What a human made is: the script,
dialogue, the selection and order of images, the page layouts, and images substantially reworked
by a person. Many other countries require "the author's own intellectual creation" in a similar way.
In practice: keep the script, all prompts and versions (documentation of your own creative work),
rework images where you can, and disclose AI use where a publisher or platform asks (Amazon KDP
does). This is not legal advice; check with a publisher or lawyer before a big release.

Never imitate a living artist's signature style by name, and never use other people's characters,
logos or panels.

## Print

- Page count divisible by 4 (add blank, title or colophon pages).
- `--bleed 3` gives 3 mm bleed; keep text at least 5 mm from the trim (the builder uses 9–13 mm margins).
  With `--bleed`, images are used at full resolution.
- Images: at least 300 dpi at print size. A 146 mm wide panel needs ~1700 px. Upscale small ones.
- Cover for print: front + spine + back with 3 mm bleed; the printer gives you the spine width.
- Formats: US graphic novel 6.625 × 10.25 in (`us`), Franco-Belgian album ~240 × 320 mm
  (`album`), manga/small ~A5 (`a5`), `a4`.
- ISBN: from your national ISBN agency or through the publisher. Put it in the colophon and on the back.

## Reading online

- `flipbook.py` turns the page PNGs into one self-contained HTML flip-book (cover alone, then
  two-page spreads). Host it anywhere, or share it as a file.
- A version **without lettering** (empty all `text` lists in a copy of comic.json and build it
  with `--variant "no text"`) is handy for translations and for editing in a layout program.
- Translation: copy comic.json, translate only the `text` fields, build with a new variant –
  the images stay the same.
